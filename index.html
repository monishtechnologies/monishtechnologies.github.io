The snack signal

My robot is out of power. The science fair starts in an hour!
🤖🪫
No charger. No spare battery. One worried inventor.
Maybe this cookie will help? It's all I've got.
🤖🍪CRUNCH!
The robot eats the cookie. That was not in the manual.
POWER LEVEL: DELICIOUS.
🤖⚡ZAP!
It works! But the robot's antenna starts flashing.
Why is my radio beeping back?
🤖📻BEEP!
The cookie didn't just charge the robot. It sent a signal.
That signal is coming from... the Moon?!
🌕📡PING!
Something far away answers the robot's message.
Your cookie called our bakery. Need a refill?
🛸🍪WHOOSH!
A spaceship lands outside, full of alien bakers.
Great. Can you deliver to the science fair?
🤖🍪TA-DA!
First prize: a robot that can order its own snacks.
02 / 05

Puddle jumper

It's just a tiny puddle. I can jump across.
🥾💧
A kid takes a shortcut home after the rain.
Where did the sidewalk go?!
🌊🥾SPLASH!
The puddle is a doorway to an underwater kingdom.
Our royal crown is missing! The sea is draining!
🐠🏰GLUB!
A fish begs the kid to help save its home.
Wait... what's stuck under my boot?
🥾👑POP!
The boot accidentally pulled the crown away on landing.
That crown is also the plug for our sea!
👑🌀GURGLE!
Without it, water pours out of the kingdom.
Quick! Put it back where I landed!
🥾👑PLONK!
The kid plugs the hole. The sea fills up again.
All hail the Royal Boot!
🥾🐚HOORAY!
Back on the sidewalk, the kid finds a seashell medal on that boot.
03 / 05

The runaway book

One more chapter...
📖😴
A kid stays up late reading a favorite book.
Hey! Come back with my story!
📖WHOOSH!
The book flies out the window. The kid grabs hold.
A book with rocket engines?!
🚀📖BOOM!
The book blasts off with the kid still holding on.
Welcome to the Moon Library. We need help!
🌕📚THUD!
Every book in the library is missing its ending.
Who stole the endings? ...Wait, these pages are blank!
📄🔍GASP!
Nobody stole them. The Moon Library has no one to write them.
So you weren't running away. You came to find a writer!
🧒📖DING!
The flying book picked the kid for the job.
Once upon a time, a book flew to the Moon...
✏️📖TA-DA!
The first story the kid finishes is the one you just read.
04 / 05

The shy thundercloud

Don't sneeze. The parade is starting below!
☁️🌼
At sunset, a shy cloud drifts over a town parade.
Aaaa... CHOO!
🌩️KABOOM!
Its sneeze makes lightning strike. The parade lights go out.
I ruined everything. I'll hide behind this roof.
☁️🏘️
The street is dark, and the cloud feels terrible.
That boom sounded like our drum! We came to help!
✨🏮TWINKLE!
Fireflies follow the thunder and light the street.
Oh no. Now I'm raining on everyone!
🌧️💧SPLASH!
The raindrops catch the glow of the fireflies.
Look! The sunset is making a rainbow!
🌈✨TA-DA!
The whole town stops to watch the sky.
Lead the parade! Your thunder can be the beat!
🌩️🥁BOOM!
The cloud stops hiding and leads the brightest parade yet.
05 / 05

Mosquito Saver

What's that little buzzing sound?
🧑🦟BUZZ!
Monish spots a mosquito resting on a leaf.
I drank too much blood. I'm too full to fly!
🦟🩸
Its wings flap, but its heavy belly keeps it on the leaf.
Hold on. I think I can help you.
🧑💉AHA!
Monish finds a tiny syringe in his science kit.
Just a little of that extra blood...
💉🦟SLOORP!
With the mosquito's okay, Monish gently draws off a little blood.
I can lift my wings again! Wait... not quite!
🦟🍃FLAP!
The mosquito rises an inch, then lands. One wing is stuck to the leaf.
It's only a drop of sticky juice. You're free!
🧑🦟POP!
Monish lifts the leaf away from the wing without hurting it.
I'M FLYING! Thanks, Monish! Next time, smaller sips!
🦟👋ZOOM!
The mosquito loops through the air and flies home after it drank some of monish's blood. Monish waves badbye. [LOL] 😂
